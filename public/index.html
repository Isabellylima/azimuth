FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where do the sailing tours depart from?

All Lagos tours depart from Marina de Lagos, in the Algarve, Portugal. Atlantic sea crossings depart from Lagos or Porto Santo, and the Azores programs start in Terceira.

Are the tours private?

Yes. Lagos tours and charters are private: the yacht Angela is exclusively for your group, with up to 10 guests on day trips and up to 8 guests sleeping on board.

How does booking work?

Choose a program and a date in the calendar and send your request. We confirm availability by email, and the booking is secured with a 50% advance payment; the balance is due one month before the trip.

Do I need sailing experience for a sea crossing?

Tell us your experience when you request a place. On crossings you become part of the crew and learn with an experienced skipper: watches, sail handling, navigation and weather.

What happens if the weather is bad?

Safety comes first. The skipper decides whether it is safe to sail and, if not, we agree an alternative plan or date with you.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancellations made 30 or more days before the trip are charged up to 50%; less than 30 days, up to 100%. See the full Terms & Conditions.

Can I book by WhatsApp?

Yes — use the WhatsApp button on any page and we reply personally.
