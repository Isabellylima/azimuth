Lagos Private Boat Trip – Half Day & Full Day Sailing Tours

If you are searching for a private boat trip in Lagos, you’ve just found the ultimate way to experience the Algarve coast. Whether you prefer a half-day sailing tour or a full-day private yacht charter, our sailing experiences offer comfort, exclusivity, and unforgettable views.

Why Choose a Private Boat Trip in Lagos?

A private sailing tour is the perfect choice if you want to enjoy the beauty of the Algarve without the crowds. From the moment you step aboard our yacht, it’s just you, your group, and the ocean.

  • Ideal for families, couples, or groups of friends.

  • Celebrate special occasions in style – birthdays, anniversaries, or a stag/hen party.

  • Discover hidden caves and golden beaches only accessible by boat.

Half-Day Private Sailing Tour

Perfect if you’re short on time but still want to enjoy the magic of Lagos by sea. A half-day tour lets you sail along the coast, anchor for a swim or stand-up paddle session, and relax on deck with drinks and snacks.


Full-Day Private Yacht Charter

For a truly immersive experience, choose a full-day sailing tour. Spend the entire day at sea, exploring more of the Algarve’s dramatic cliffs and secret coves. Take time to swim, sunbathe, and enjoy every moment on board.

This option is ideal for those who want a luxury private yacht experience in Lagos.

A Tailored Experience Just for You

Our private charters are designed around your preferences. Whether you want a relaxed day of sailing, an active adventure with water sports, or a romantic escape, our crew will make it happen.
