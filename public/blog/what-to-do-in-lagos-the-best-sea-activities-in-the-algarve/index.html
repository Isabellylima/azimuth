What to Do in Lagos: The Best Sea Activities in the Algarve

Lagos is one of the most popular destinations in the Algarve — and for good reason. With its golden cliffs, turquoise waters, and relaxed atmosphere, it’s the perfect place for travellers who want to enjoy the sea. If you’re wondering what to do in Lagos, here are some of the best sea activities that you can’t miss during your stay.

1. Go on a Sailing Tour in Lagos

There’s nothing quite like exploring the Algarve coastline from the deck of a sailing yacht. From half-day boat tours to full-day charters and romantic sunset cruises, sailing is one of the most unique ways to see Lagos. You’ll enjoy stunning views of Ponta da Piedade, hidden caves, and quiet beaches — all while relaxing with drinks and snacks on board.

👉 Looking for something truly special? Azimuth Holidays also offers a Sea Crossing from Lagos to Porto Santo (Madeira Islands), a multi-day adventure for those who want to experience life at sea.

2. Stand Up Paddle (SUP) Along the Coast

If you love being active on the water, SUP is a fantastic way to explore the calm bays and dramatic cliffs of Lagos. Many boat tours, including Azimuth’s private charters, provide SUP boards so you can paddle at your own pace and discover hidden corners of the coastline.

3. Snorkelling in Crystal-Clear Waters

Lagos is home to beautiful marine life and clear waters that are perfect for snorkelling. Combine a sailing trip with snorkelling stops and enjoy the Algarve’s underwater beauty while you swim freely in the Atlantic.

4. Watch the Sunset From the Ocean

One of the most magical experiences in Lagos is a sunset sailing tour. Watching the sun dip below the horizon from the comfort of a yacht, with a glass of wine in hand, is an unforgettable highlight of any trip to the Algarve.

5. Enjoy a Full-Day at Sea With Family & Friends

For groups or families, a private full-day sailing charter is the perfect way to celebrate together. Spend the day swimming, sunbathing, paddle boarding, and enjoying the best views of Lagos — all at your own pace, with a professional crew taking care of everything.

Final Thoughts: Lagos Sea Activities You’ll Never Forget

Whether you’re looking for adventure, relaxation, or a mix of both, the best memories in Lagos are made at sea. From sailing tours to SUP, snorkelling, and ocean sunsets, there’s something for everyone to enjoy.
