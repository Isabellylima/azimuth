Boat Tour Lagos: Discover the Best Sailing Experience in the Algarve

If you are looking for the ultimate boat tour in Lagos, you’ve just found it! Sailing in the Algarve is one of the most unforgettable ways to explore the region, and our classic sailing yacht Angela offers a unique and elegant way to enjoy the sea, the sun, and the stunning cliffs of Lagos.

Departing from Marina de Lagos, our sailing tours combine adventure and relaxation. Picture yourself gliding across crystal-clear waters, surrounded by the dramatic golden cliffs and hidden caves that make the Algarve famous worldwide. Whether you’re looking for a relaxing afternoon at sea, a romantic sunset sailing tour in Lagos, or even a full-day private experience with family and friends, there’s a tour designed for you.

Why choose a sailing tour in Lagos?

  • Unique perspective: From the deck of our yacht, you’ll see the coastline like never before.

  • Comfort & style: Our boat provides a spacious, elegant atmosphere with all the essentials for a perfect day.

  • Unforgettable memories: From stand-up paddle (SUP) to swimming stops and enjoying a glass of wine on board, every moment is designed for you to make the most of your time in the Algarve.
