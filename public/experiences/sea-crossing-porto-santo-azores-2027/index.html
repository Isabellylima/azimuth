Sea Crossing: Porto Santo → Azores

Five days of deep blue between Madeira and São Miguel.

  • ⏱ 5 days sailing + 2 days in Ponta Delgada
  • 👥 Up to 5 guests
  • 📍 Porto Santo → Ponta Delgada

Continue the Atlantic journey from Porto Santo to Ponta Delgada, on São Miguel Island in the Azores. Five days of open-ocean sailing, followed by two days in Ponta Delgada to discover the landscapes, culture and atmosphere of the Azores.

Check-in 16 April 2027, departure 17 April 2027. Maximum 5 guests; meals on board included.

What’s included

  • Accommodation in a cabin on board

  • Safety equipment and briefing
