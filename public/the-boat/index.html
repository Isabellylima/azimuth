Angela

Your floating home on the Atlantic — luxurious, spacious and made for unforgettable moments at sea.

Step aboard our 15-metre sailing yacht — one of the most spacious of its kind — equipped for both short coastal cruises and extended sea adventures.

With 5 cabins (3 double and 2 twin), 3 bathrooms and accommodation for up to 8 overnight guests (10 for day charters), Angela blends comfort, functionality and style. The saloon is bright and airy, with a large seating area and dining table.
